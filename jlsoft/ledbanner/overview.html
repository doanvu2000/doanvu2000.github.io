Ads & Monetization

🚀

App Open Ad

Shown on cold launch (Splash) and app resume. Keys: AOA_SPLASH, AOA_RESUME.

📺

Interstitial

Intro flow + template/favorite open tap. Keys: INTER_INTRO, INTER_DEFAULT.

🖼️

Native Ads

Language (250dp), Intro 1/2 + full-in-list, Home bottom (collapsible), Template between sections.

👑

Premium

Monthly subscription + Lifetime one-time. Google Play Billing. Disables all ads globally.

Master switch: appConfig().isShowAllAds — if false, all ad logic short-circuits before any unit check.
